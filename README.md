One line of an out-of-office auto-reply, the funniest I could make it. Written for make.fun's "The Funniest Out-of-Office" bounty.

## The line

> I am out of the office until Monday, and so is my will to live — please email someone who still has one.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program prints exactly one line and writes the same line (no trailing newline) to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Why this line

It works because it follows the auto-reply formula exactly — out until Monday, redirect to a colleague — and then the thing that is out is not the person but their will to live. The redirect is the punch: the sender is sent to "someone who still has one", which is both helpful office etiquette and a bleak little confession. It stays in one line, in the voice of a real auto-reply, with no setup the reader has to wade through.
